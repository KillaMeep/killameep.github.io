I'm a developer focused on Python and machine learning. Most of my time goes into computer vision and AI with frameworks like OpenCV, PyTorch and TensorFlow, building tools that solve real problems through automation.

Beyond Python I work in Rust, JavaScript and C#, and I build desktop apps with Tauri and Electron. That lets me take an idea from a model or a script all the way to something people can download and run.
